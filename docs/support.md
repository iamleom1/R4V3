# R4V3 Support

Last updated: April 23, 2026

## Contact

For closed beta support, contact the R4V3 team through the TestFlight feedback channel or the support email listed in App Store Connect.

## What to include

- Account email used in the beta.
- Device model and iOS version.
- A short description of what happened.
- The screen or tester-script step where the issue occurred.
- A screenshot or screen recording when possible.

## Account deletion

Users can delete their account in the app from the Profile screen. Account deletion removes the profile and associated app data, including photos, RSVPs, matches, and messages.

## Safety

Use in-app reporting for safety, harassment, spam, or impersonation issues. For urgent safety concerns, contact local emergency services first.
